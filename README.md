## One‑line pitch
Paste your numbers and goals → get clear, comparable recommendations on refinance vs. offset vs. invest, tailored for NZ/Aus property owners.

- Mortgages and refinancing choices

**“Paste your numbers → get clear recommendations.”**

# Define the product precisely

## Exact user
- 28–45 years old
- New homeowner or buying a second home
- Busy, financially literate, wants clarity not spreadsheets
- Located in NZ/Aus with local bank products

## Exact pain
- “I don’t know the best move for my mortgage right now.”
- “I can’t compare upgrade options without a spreadsheet rabbit hole.”
- “I need a confident answer with trade‑offs, not generic advice.”

## Exact outcome
- A clear ranked recommendation with transparent assumptions
- A simple action plan: refinance, change offset, upgrade, invest, or wait

# MVP features (no fluff)
1. **Guided input form** (mortgage, rates, equity, reno, solar, upgrade assumptions)
2. **Scenario engine** (refi vs. offset vs. upgrade vs. invest)
3. **Cashflow + net worth comparison** (1, 3, 5 years)
4. **Sensitivity sliders** (rate change, inflation, value uplift assumptions)
5. **Action checklist** (next steps, questions to ask bank or builder)
6. **PDF summary** (share with partner or broker)
